4. What Problem Are We Solving?

Many students struggle to find genuine virtual internships that match their skills and interests. Internship details are often spread across different websites, making it difficult for students to discover and apply for opportunities. Companies also face challenges in managing internship applications, assigning tasks, and tracking student progress.

The Virtual Internship Platform solves these problems by providing a centralized web application where students can search and apply for internships, while companies can manage internship programs efficiently. This platform simplifies the entire internship process for both students and recruiters.

5. Proposed Solution

The Virtual Internship Platform is a web application that connects students and companies on a single platform. Students can register, create their profiles, upload resumes, browse available internships, and apply online. Companies can post internship opportunities, review applications, assign tasks, evaluate student performance, and provide certificates. The admin manages users, verifies companies, and monitors the overall system. The platform makes the internship process faster, transparent, and more organized.

8. Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful if:

Students can register and log in successfully.
Students can search and apply for internships easily.
Companies can post internship opportunities.
Companies can review and shortlist applications.
Students can submit assigned internship tasks.
Certificates are generated after successful internship completion.
Admin can manage users and internship postings without errors.
9. Out of Scope

The first version (MVP) will not include:

Video conferencing
Live chat between students and mentors
AI-based internship recommendation
Online coding assessment
Payment gateway
Mobile application
Blockchain certificate verification
Multi-language support

These features can be added in future versions.
